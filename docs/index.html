This page compares how much predictive signal for echocardiogram-confirmed structural heart disease (SHD) can be pulled from a 12-lead ECG, as more information is added: starting from demographics alone (age, sex, race/ethnicity, care setting), then adding standard ECG measurements (heart rate, PR/QRS/QT intervals), then hand-crafted waveform features, their combination, and finally a 1D convolutional neural network trained directly on the raw waveform. Each dot is one disease target; AUROC (area under the ROC curve) measures how well a model ranks people with the condition above people without it — 0.5 is a coin flip, 1.0 is perfect separation. These are provisional validation-split results, assembled automatically because reports/final_results.csv (the held-out test-split summary with bootstrap confidence intervals) has not been produced yet.
